Crop / focus area

Draw a rectangle with the Crop tool, then Focus: tracing and the DXF use only that part of the picture (DXF origin = crop corner). Crop again to narrow further.

No crop

Assess whole crop

Crop lighting often differs from the rest of the picture. Re-trace the entire cropped section with its own local settings (independent of the Processing sliders). The size reference above keeps working.

Re-scan area

Missed a cut? Choose the Re-scan tool and drag a rectangle over the spot. That region alone is re-traced from the source pixels with its own settings; the green preview shows what would be found. Add to cuts merges it into the cuts (undo available).

Merge touching cuts

Contours that touch, overlap or lie within the tolerance are unioned into one outline (shared inner edges removed, holes kept).

5. Zones

Choose the Zones tool and drag on the image to draw a rectangle. Drag a zone to move it, drag its handles to resize. A zone that is switched off removes every contour whose centre (centroid or bounding-box centre) is inside it from the DXF.
